## 2026-11-27 — Phase 7 build (Inside store & kitchen)
- **Two new slices,** `state.inside` and `state.kitchen` (types in `src/core/insideTypes.ts`), read through `insideOf` / `kitchenOf` so books saved before Phase 7 still open; `SCHEMA` stays at 2. Reset to the demo data to see the Phase 7 demo.
- **Departments are configurable** (name, revenue account, cost-of-goods account, expected cost %, shrink threshold, POS names). Defaults: beer & wine, tobacco, grocery & snacks, beverages, other merchandise (the last three share 5120), lottery (4140 commissions, not counted, not on the POS close) and kitchen (4200/5200).
- **The POS department import posts nothing.** The POS daily close already put the sales in the books; the import adds units and the customer count and must tie to the close, department by department. Idempotent per store + day + POS department; one corrected file means undoing the earlier batch. Average ticket = inside + kitchen sales on days with a customer count ÷ customers.
- **Purchases are read from the ledger** (department cost-of-goods accounts, inventory-count journals left out), so checks, EFT, cash and approved bills all count. Vendor mappings assign departments; a multi-department mapping (or a shared account like 5120) splits by that store's department sales that month. Bills awaiting approval count as committed against purchasing budgets.
- **Inventory is periodic.** A posted count moves 1310 at the store to the counted total: per department counted − last count, against that department's cost-of-goods account, so cost of goods = purchases − inventory increase. Anything in 1310 the counts don't explain (an opening balance, a manual entry) is spread across the counted departments. A partial count leaves other departments at their last count. Store managers enter counts; posting needs `inside.edit`. Counts post in date order; only the latest can be voided (its journal is soft-deleted).
- **Book inventory** = last count + purchases − sales × expected cost %; **shrink** = book − counted, measured as a % of the department's sales since the last count. The kitchen department's shrink is shown but alerts through food cost, not the shrink inbox line.
- **Food cost** = kitchen purchases − kitchen inventory increase, which always equals 5200 in the ledger. A month counts as measured only with a kitchen count posted on or after the 25th. Target per store, alert when more than ±2 points away on the latest measured month. Waste is logged, not posted.
- **Kitchen labor** = payroll's Kitchen department by employee, for pay weeks whose Friday pay date falls in the month (a simplification, like labor %).
- **Scan-data integrates with Receivables:** a program period is linked to one Receivables rebate item; claiming writes the scan-data expected amount onto it, and receipts are applied there. Short-paid = received but at least $5 below expected.
- **Lottery** is a memo subledger (agency sales; nothing posts). Shortage = register lottery net at least $5 below sales − payouts. Commissions on weeks ending in a month tie to 4140.
- **Supplier price alerts:** price before = the last invoice at a different price; the top 15 items by spend alert over 5% either way until reviewed. Invoices are matched to purchases by vendor, store and total within 7 days.
- **New permissions:** `inside.edit` (CFO, bookkeeper: imports, mappings, budgets, programs, posting counts, targets) and `inside.log` (also store managers, own store: counts, lottery weeks, age checks, waste). Margin, budget, rebate and kitchen report screens need `view.reports`.
- **Demo data** (illustrative): POS department detail for every POS close through 25 Nov (26 Nov is left for the import), opening counts that agree with 1310 and month-end counts for Sep and Oct with HM13 tobacco shrink at 3.4%, November purchasing budgets with HM15 tobacco over, Reynolds and ITG scan-data programs linked to the Phase 5 rebates (Reynolds Q3 short-paid by $279.18, ITG overdue), weekly lottery with an HM13 shortage the week ending 14 Nov, age-check audits with one open failure, monthly menu mix for each kitchen, Sysco invoice detail for every Sysco purchase with the wings price jumping 13% from 12 Nov, a waste log, and HM15's October food cost at 41% vs a 36% target. Nothing touches Trustmark, so the bank feed and reconciliations are unchanged. Reynolds' Phase 5 receivable now expects the scan-data figure ($3,519.78) instead of the amount paid.
- **Not yet:** live POS (PDI / CStorePro) and Sysco connections, item-level inside inventory (counts are by department at cost), perpetual inventory and accrual merchandise COGS in the P&L (Phase 4), lottery settlement postings, and license expiry (Phase 9).
## 2026-11-27 — Phase 6 build (People & payroll)
- **Posting follows SPEC §6.7:** per store, net pay → `6100`, employee + employer taxes → `6110`, on the pay date, crediting Trustmark (CORP). The Gusto debit line equals the run's *total company debit* (direct-deposit net + all taxes). Paper checks on a Gusto run aren't in Gusto's debit, so each posts on its own with its check number and shows in the checks register. A run can only post when its lines tie to the report's totals, and never into a locked month.
- **Demo runs are attached, not re-posted.** Each weekly `GUSTO PAYROLL <store>` entry the core demo already posted got a run whose employee lines add to it to the cent (`txnIds` points at it), so the Phase 2 bank feed and reconciliations are unchanged. The 27 Nov runs are imported but not posted: that's the week waiting for review.
- **Import is idempotent by line:** key = pay date + Gusto id (or name) + gross + net, numbered within the file. One pay date per import. Unknown employees are added and wait in "New hires to confirm". Deductions aren't supported yet (gross must equal net + employee taxes).
- **Pay weeks run Saturday–Friday**, named by the Friday pay date. Weekly review, the cash/Gusto comparison and labor % all use this week. Labor % compares the pay week's labor with the same week's POS sales (a simplification: Gusto may pay a period in arrears).
- **Labor cost = gross + employer taxes + cash/check pay**, which is the same as net + all taxes + cash/check, i.e. 6100 + 6110 in the books once posted. Labor % targets are per store (demo: HM11 6.5%, HM13 9.5%, HM15 7.5%, HM17 12%, of total POS sales including fuel).
- **Cash and check pay outside Gusto** is one entry per employee and pay week. Linking to an existing cash-chain payroll payout posts nothing new and can't exceed the payout. **Never assumed to duplicate Gusto:** paid both ways in a week shows "Both" until someone confirms what the extra pay was for.
- **The 15% flag** fires when labor cost moves more than 15% either way against the prior pay week (strictly over 15%). A store with nothing yet this week shows "Not in yet" rather than −100%.
- **Rate changes** need `payroll.edit` to request (CFO, bookkeeper) and `approve` to approve (CFO, owner). A rejection needs a reason. The current rate is the starting rate plus approved changes effective by that date. The review warns when Gusto pays a rate that isn't approved.
- **New permission `payroll.edit`** (CFO, bookkeeper). Payroll screens need `view.reports`; store managers don't see payroll.
- **Payroll taxes:** liability = employee + employer taxes on every run, split federal / state using the run's state-tax column. Demo: Gusto deposits federal on each pay date and state monthly; Q3 941 and state returns are marked filed.
- **Older saved books** without the `people` slice still open (read through `peopleOf`; written on first payroll change), so `SCHEMA` stays at 2.
- **"Payments with no document"** still leaves out payroll: the Gusto run is the document.
- **Not yet:** a live Gusto API connection (needs the hosted back end and OAuth), deductions/benefits and garnishments, payroll accruals at month end (phase 8/10), per-entity 941s (the tracker totals all employers), and PDF pay stubs.
## 2026-11-27 — Phase 3 build (Fuel)
- **Fuel is a subledger** (`state.fuel`): invoices/BOLs, Craddock notices, Dash statements, structured daily fuel sales, the Comdata summary, Mudflap entries, tanks and readings, posted and competitor prices. Everything that touches the books posts through the fuel functions in `src/core/fuel.ts`, which call `books.createTransaction`. Books saved before phase 3 get an empty fuel slice (`fuelOf`); the schema number is unchanged.
- **Craddock notice signs:** amounts are from our side (+ Craddock owes us). Proceeds and drafts are +; fees, host discounts, invoices and deposits are −; OTHER keeps the file's sign. A notice must net to zero to post.
- **The notice doesn't post card proceeds.** The POS close already put card sales into 1100. The notice relieves the receivable through its fees, invoices, network fees and the draft or deposit, which together equal the proceeds. The receivable at any date = opening + POS card − posted relief, and it must equal the ledger.
- **Dating (SPEC §6.2):** fees and host discounts on the sales day (processing date − 1); invoices and network fees on the notice date; the draft or deposit on the next Federal Reserve business day. A notice's entries are validated together, so it posts completely or not at all.
- **An invoice line with no invoice on file still posts** (to 5000) and is flagged as a missing invoice. An amount that disagrees with the invoice on file blocks posting.
- **Fuel invoices hit the books when they are paid:** Craddock's on the notice that nets them, Dash's from Dash clearing on their due date. Account 5000 is paid fuel, the cash-basis cost. Accrual fuel COGS comes from `fuelCostOfSales(state, storeId, period)`: gallons sold by grade × that month's delivered cost per gallon at the store (product + freight + taxes, with freight and taxes spread by net gallons), Plus blended 50/50, rounded once per grade. With no delivery of a grade that month it falls back to the store's latest earlier month, then to the company-wide month, and it says which one it used. Duplicates and BOLs without an invoice are left out of the cost basis. Fuel inventory (1300) is not adjusted yet; that belongs with the Phase 4 accrual P&L.
- **Dash weekly statement** (demo): card fees and a network fee come out of clearing. Dash remits the balance above a $6,000 working balance plus the invoices due in the next 7 days, or drafts the shortfall.
- **Truck lane:** HM15 diesel gallons and sales come from the Comdata summary, stored apart from the POS sales. The POS close still posts the revenue, and the tie-out proves the two agree. Mudflap discounts and true-ups post to 4910 against the Fiserv / AccessOne clearing (1120), so merchant deposits are net of them.
- **Tanks:** the variance allowance is 1% of gallons sold + 130 gallons over 30 days (the EPA monthly inventory-control test). Water over 1 inch is an alert. Reorder at 2.5 days of supply. A transport is 8,800 gallons; safe fill is 90%. These live in `state.fuel.targets`, as do the cpg margin band (18–45¢) and the 5% price-outlier test.
- **Break-even cpg** = discounts + card fees per gallon: the margin fuel needs before it earns anything. Card fees include inside-store cards, because suppliers charge them on the whole batch. Fixed-cost break-even comes with Phase 8.
- **Demo:** fuel loads now follow tank levels (a load when a tank is under 30% or 2.5 days of supply), prices step down through the autumn, and HM17 is priced low after opening, so its margin sits below target. One HM13 notice (23 Nov) is left unposted, with its deposit already on the bank feed. One BOL has no invoice, one Dash invoice is keyed twice, one HM11 premium line is 9% over the month, the HM13 premium tank loses 22 gal a day from 8 Nov, and the HM11 regular tank shows 1.4" of water from 24 Nov. Craddock notices are dated Mondays, so their bank lines still fall on Tuesdays and the bank feed and the September/October reconciliations behave as before; the amounts changed with the new netting.
- **Not yet:** PDF notice parsing (paste or CSV instead), live Craddock, Dash (myMPC) or Comdata feeds, ATG integration, and fuel inventory valuation.
## 2026-11-27 — Phase 5 build (Intercompany, payables & receivables)
- **Party ledgers are read from the ledger**, not kept separately: lines on intercompany accounts (1910 loans, new **1920 bills paid for related parties**, 2400 due to related parties), loan and receivable accounts, tagged with the party (on the line or the transaction). Sign: + they owe us, − we owe them. Store entities get the same ledger from Corporate's automatic 1900/2900 lines. Aging settles the oldest items first.
- **Paying another business's bill** posts to 1920 under that party, never to store expenses or the P&L.
- **Tenants** appear in the party list with the rent schedule's monthly charges (memo only, not posted: rent stays cash basis) against rent received.
- **Rent pass-throughs are templates** (`state.intercompany.rentTemplates`); the demo's main loop now calls `generateRent` on the 1st, producing the same entries (same refs) as before. Generation is idempotent per template and month.
- **Owner draws** are found on draws-type accounts and on the owner-withdrawal expense account where an owner is named, so the view works under either policy.
- **Limits** are per party (or entity), on the absolute balance; the inbox alerts above them. Demo: Southern Eagle is over.
- **Elimination view** nets only the automatic entity due-to / due-from (1900/2900). Related parties outside the group are real balances and stay.
- **Bills** are a subledger (`state.payables.bills`). A draft posts nothing; **approval** posts expense lines → 2000 on the bill date and needs the `approve` permission (owner, CFO). When the approver's role can't post journals (owner), the entry is posted under the person who entered the bill and the approval is audited under the approver. **Payment** posts 2000 → Trustmark (or cash at HQ) and needs the new `bills.pay` permission (CFO, bookkeeper); `bills.enter` covers CFO, bookkeeper and store managers (own store only). New `ar.edit` covers receivables. Amount limits by role come in phase 14.
- **One payment per bill** for now (no partial payments). Voiding an approved bill soft-deletes its accrual with the reason.
- **Forecast hook:** `addForecastSource` in `forecast.ts` lets a module add known events; approved and scheduled bills are added on their pay date and replace the nearest occurrence (within 10 days) of the same vendor's detected pattern, so nothing counts twice.
- **The bills inbox has no OCR.** Pasted text and file names are read with regular expressions; files are kept as attachments. A real OCR or email-forwarding service needs the hosted back end.
- **Utilities history** combines bills on the utility account and autopay drafts paid straight from the bank (no bill). A jump is a bill more than 25% above the one before.
- **Vendor statements** are reconciled by invoice number; every difference is classified and the classifications add to the difference exactly.
- **1099** = money out of bank or cash accounts on transactions naming a 1099 vendor, calendar year, flagged at $600.
- **Receivables** (rebates, insurance claims, refunds) are tracked as expected amounts, not accrued; receipts post money in to the item's income account or apply an existing deposit. ATM income is read from 4310 by month.
- **Approvals** are a provider registry (`src/core/approvals.ts`); bills are the first provider. Later phases add rate changes, write-offs and adjusting entries without touching the screen.
- **Saved books from before Phase 5** (same schema number) load without the new slices; the core reads them as empty. Reset to the demo data to see the Phase 5 demo.
- **Demo data** (illustrative, generic names): about 30 bills across 10 new demo vendors (one past due, one due in 3 days and unscheduled, one awaiting approval), 11 utility accounts (HM15 water jumps 40%, HM13 water past due with a disconnect notice), an ice-supplier statement with every difference explained, related-party activity for Cheers, Rowdy's, Bullseye and Five Rivers, a new laundromat tenant late for November, and rebates, an insurance claim and a refund outstanding.

## 2026-11-27 — Phase 2 build (Bank & cash)
- **Keep building in this repo** (CFO decision, 2026-11-27); StationBooks stays as it is.
- **HM17 opened November 2026** (CFO decision, 2026-11-27).
- **Bank lines are a separate subledger** (`state.bank.lines`). Each one matches one or more ledger lines on its bank account. Reconciliation is computed: book balance − book-only items + bank-only items = statement. It can only be saved at a $0.00 difference, and it locks the bank lines it covers.
- **Import is idempotent.** The key is the FITID when the file has one; otherwise it's account + date + amount + description, numbered within the file so two identical real lines both import. Every line keeps its import batch id.
- **Auto-match only takes clear winners:** a check-number hit, or one candidate at least 10 points ahead. Ties are left for a person.
- **Cash chain postings:** a count vs POS difference, a pickup vs count difference and an HQ vs pickup difference all go to `6340 Cash over / short` at the store. The pickup moves cash from `1010` (store) to `1020 Cash at HQ / in transit` (Corporate). The pooled deposit moves `1020` → Trustmark. Flags fire at materiality ($5.00); smaller differences are still posted.
- **HM11 cash payroll** is a payout from the HQ pool. Payouts for change are transfers (no expense). Owner withdrawals follow the Settings policy.
- **Stale checks:** 60 days (configurable). **Deposit due:** within 2 days of pickup (configurable).
- **Forecast** starts from the book balance (outstanding checks are already in it). Recurring items are detected from 13 weeks of history; the CFO can switch any off or add manual items.
- **Not yet:** PDF statement parsing, a direct bank feed (needs a provider such as Plaid and the hosted back end), and check images from the bank (attach them to the transaction for now).

## 2026-11-27 — Phase 1 build
- **Stack:** Vite + React 19 + TypeScript, plain CSS tokens ("Ledger" design), react-router (hash routes, so it works as a static page). The accounting core in `src/core` is framework-free and unit-tested.
- **Storage:** IndexedDB on one device for Phase 1. Swapping in a hosted back end (multi-user, 2FA, encryption, backups) is a prerequisite before real data goes in.
- **Intercompany is automatic.** Each store has its own entity, and Corporate is the hub. When a transaction's lines span entities, the ledger adds `2900 Due to related entities` / `1900 Due from related entities` lines so each entity balances. Example: the shared Trustmark account (CORP) pays an HM11 bill.
- **Owner withdrawals:** the default policy is *Corporate · Miscellaneous expense* (CFO decision, 2026-11-02). It can be switched to equity draws in Settings, and the policy fills in when an owner is picked on an entry.
- **Rent pass-throughs:** each one debits the store's rent and credits Corporate's *Due to related parties* (Dhunna / SKD). The demo uses round illustrative amounts. Real amounts stay out of the repo and are entered as data.
- **HM17** opened Nov 2026 per the build spec (the house notes say Oct 2026; this is configurable on the store record). **HM14** is leased out and appears only through rent income.
- **Materiality:** $5.00.
- **"Payments with no document"** is a review queue, not a to-do. It covers the last 30 days, $1,000 and over, and leaves out payroll until the Gusto import ships.
- **Demo data** is illustrative. None of it is real company figures, owner names or account numbers; those are never committed.
